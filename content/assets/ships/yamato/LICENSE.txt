Modifications by the Navy Field visual prototype project:
- Imported native 3MF files and restored print-layout assembly offsets.
- Converted coordinates and uniformly normalized hull length to 263 m.
- Removed zero-area triangles, calculated normals and assigned new materials.
- Separated original main-gun meshes into turret bodies and individual tubes.
- Added independent yaw, elevation and recoil nodes, muzzle and funnel anchors.
- Exported the assembled model as yamato.glb.
- Applied a project-generated wooden deck texture at runtime.

The source is a printable RC model, not a museum-grade reconstruction.
Its proportions are preserved: scaled hull beam is about 41.12 m rather than
the historical 38.9 m. Not every supplied accessory is assembled.
The static Musashi reference currently reuses the same model.
